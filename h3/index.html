One job, start to finish

We use Claude, Codex, Gemini, TypeSafe, GitHub and Cloudflare in our daily work. No partnership or endorsement is implied.

  1. You ask. Leave a voicemail at (262) 315-2739, or send an email. Say it the way you would say it to a person.
  2. Your team lead picks it up: an AI agent that turns what you said into a clear ticket.
  3. The project lead scopes it. The job is cut into pieces, and every piece goes up on The Rail, the one line every crew works from.
  4. Crews build the pieces. Mixed crews of specialists work side by side. Know-how comes from The Library, which grows as we work. Your details come from your own files, kept separate from The Library.
  5. The Sheepdogs keep it moving. They fetch pieces off The Rail, carry know-how up from The Library and details up from your files, and bring back anything that drifts. Before anything reaches you, a reviewer who did not build it checks it.
  6. The pieces are fitted and tested. The leads put the work together, check that it reads as one piece, and test it on a phone and a desktop.
  7. It comes back to you. A text to say it is ready and an email with the finished work. Not quite right? Say so, and it goes round again.
