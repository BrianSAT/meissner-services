How to hand us a job

A short narrated walkthrough of the form on our home page.

  1. This is the Meissner.Services home page. Here is how to hand the team a job.
  2. Under "What you can hand us", ideas drift past, from small to large. Pick one, and it lands in the first box.
  3. Or write your own. Say what would help, in your own words. A sentence is enough.
  4. Next, name what you would pay for it. We don't set a price, and we don't charge by the hour.
  5. The bar shows how your number splits: twenty-five percent to start, and seventy-five percent when you're happy.
  6. If you like, name the tools you'd like us to use. We can work in your tools and connect to your accounts.
  7. Then pick your timing. Whatever you pick, the answer is the same: we only work fast.
  8. "Send it to the team" opens an email with everything you wrote. Nothing goes anywhere until you press send in your own mail.
  9. We answer the same day, in writing. If we can do it for your price, we say yes. If we can't, we say no.
  10. Further down, the page keeps its own record: when it was asked for, built, tested and checked.

CHORES The things that eat your day

A voicemail became a birdseed order

  1. Nov 5. The founder's mom leaves a voicemail. She needs birdseed.
  2. We text back one question: pickup or delivery, and how many bags?
  3. She answers by text.
  4. We find the bag, put it in the cart, and text her the item and the price.
  5. She answers: Yes.
  6. Nov 6. The order is placed on her own card, and we text her the confirmation.

Her part: a voicemail and two text replies. Nothing was ordered until she had said yes to the item and the price.

An inbox, sorted and kept sorted

  1. We read six months of mail: 687 conversations in 167 sender groups.
  2. We proposed 18 clean-up choices, every one unticked. The owner picked the ones she wanted by replying to an email.
  3. Ten filters went in, each read back from the mail settings to confirm it.
  4. Inbox mail older than a week is archived, not deleted, and a weekly tidy is scheduled.
