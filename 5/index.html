5.TERMS

The terms, in full

Fixed price. Fixed inputs. Everything is yours.

In plain words: the price is fixed before we start, the inputs are fixed when we say yes, and everything we make is yours. The ten terms below spell that out. None of them is a trap, and they take about two minutes to read.

Read the ten terms

The price is agreed before work starts

In writing, on the platform or by email. It does not change unless the scope changes, and a changed scope gets its own written yes before we do the extra work.

The inputs are fixed at the yes

The file, the format, the rules and the acceptance test are named when we say yes. That is what keeps the price fixed.

Questions answered, one correction round

Both are included. Questions that come up during the work get answered as they come; if the result does not match what we agreed, one correction round fixes it.

Larger jobs run in milestones

Each milestone has its own acceptance test. You release payment for a milestone when it matches what we agreed, and not before.

You own everything from day one

Code, data, documents, accounts. Work is built inside your own accounts wherever the tool allows it. There is nothing to migrate away from us later.

Hand-back is files, or your own accounts

Whatever is not built inside your accounts comes back as files in the format we agreed, with a log of what changed. We host nothing for you after hand-back, so there is no hosted view to lose and no fee to keep one alive.

Your credentials stay yours

Any access you grant is used inside your accounts for this job. We keep no credentials after hand-back, and we never ask for access we do not need.

Your data is used for this job only

It is never reused in samples or shown to anyone else. Sensitive files are handled in a private workspace for the engagement.

What we do not do

We do not give tax or legal advice, we do not file with your credentials on your behalf, and we make no claims about trading or investment performance. We build and document the tools; the decisions stay with you.

Payment and disputes

Pay through the platform you found us on, under its escrow and dispute rules, or by the method agreed at the yes. We do not ask you to go around the platform.

5.WRONG

When something goes wrong

What actually happens, in order.

If the result does not match the agreement

  1. You tell us what differs from the agreed scope, in your own words, in your channel.
  2. We fix it in the included correction round. Every change we make is logged, so you can see exactly what moved.
  3. If the fix needs something that was not in the agreed scope, we say so, and it gets its own yes or no before we do it. You decide.
  4. If we still cannot deliver what we agreed, you do not release the milestone. The platform's dispute process is there for exactly that, and we will not ask you to skip it.

If the problem is ours to prevent

  1. Numbers are checked before they reach you: counts reconcile, totals balance, every row is accounted for. A file that does not reconcile does not get sent.
  2. Anything we cannot decide with confidence is flagged for you, not guessed.
  3. A human reviews every delivery before it goes out. The agents build; Brian checks.
  4. If we are late against something we promised, you hear it from us first, with the reason and the new time.
5.FAQ

Questions people ask first

Frequently asked, plainly answered.

Who actually does the work?

A team of AI agents built around your project does the building, research and checking. Brian Meissner sets the scope and the rules, reviews every delivery and is the person you talk to. That is the whole team; there is no one else to introduce.

Is the price really fixed?

Yes, for the scope we said yes to. The inputs are fixed at that yes so the price can be. If you change the scope, the difference gets its own yes or no before we do it, and either of us can say no.

How fast will I get something?

A working first version comes back within 24 to 72 hours of your inputs for most small jobs, because the build runs in agent time; that range is what our October 2026 proposals name. We do not print one number for every job until we have measured it on our own runs. Your review rounds usually set the pace, and they are yours to pace. Anything else that delays a job, you hear about from us first.

Which tools and languages do you work in?

Yours. Spreadsheets, databases, Python, APIs, the automation and no-code tools you already pay for, your cloud account. We work in English and Spanish; Portuguese conversationally. Tool names on our pages are tools we work in, never clients.

Do you keep my data or my credentials?

No. Your data is used for this job only and is never reused in samples. Access you grant is used inside your accounts and we keep no credentials after hand-back. Everything we hand back is yours to keep.

Can you show me past work?

We do not publish client work, names or testimonials, and we will not invent any. What we can show is a sample built on synthetic data in the shape of your job, labelled as such, and this site, which the same team built overnight.

I subcontract for agencies. Can you be invisible?

Yes. Deliverables go to you, in your formats, with your name on them if you like. We work in the hours and channels you set.

What will you not do?

Tax or legal advice. Filing with your credentials on your behalf. Trading or investment performance claims. Work that needs your credentials outside your own accounts. Guessing when the honest answer is a question.

5.LOGO

The mark, design five

How we work, on any task

  1. Prototype. We build working options for your task, not slides about it.
  2. Rate. You rate each part in echoes, separately for style and for copy, from 1 to 5. One rating per page is enough to move forward; rate more if you like.
  3. Synthesize. One button turns your ratings into the next version. A first draft appears straight away in your browser, built mechanically from your ratings. Our refined version follows: the crew smooths the copy and fixes anything the mechanical merge got wrong, and the page tells you when it's ready.
  4. Repeat. Rate the new version, drag parts into a new order, hide them or swap in the runner-up. Go round again until it's right.
  5. Deliver. You get the finished work in your tools and formats, at the price agreed before we started.

The same loop works on any task:

  • Database cleanup: sample rows cleaned different ways, and the matching rules shown as options. You rate them, and the full cleanup follows the rules you chose.
  • Research presentation: slide options with different structures and visual styles. The final deck takes the copy and the style you rated highest.
  • Interactive website: like this site. You rate seven designs part by part, and the next version combines what you liked.
