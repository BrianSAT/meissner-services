How it works

Watch the walkthrough once, or read the same steps in writing. Press "Show me" on a step and the video jumps there.

  1. Sign in

    The first time, Echo asks who you are. Choose "Sign in with Email", type the email address our invitation came to, and press "Send magic link". A message from signin@meissner.services arrives with a link. Open it and you're in. The link lasts 15 minutes; if it runs out, ask for another the same way.

    you@example.comSend magic link

  2. Look around

    Echo opens on "Live". Your copy works like the real site, so scroll around and try it.

    LiveReview

  3. Switch to Review

    When you have something to say, press "Review". The page holds still and a panel opens beside it.

    LiveReview

  4. Click the part you mean

    A numbered pin lands right where you clicked, and the panel shows that part.

    Yours is ready now.1

  5. Rate it, if you like

    "Style" is the look and "Copy" is the words. 1 is "not for me", 5 is "love it". Skip either one.

    12345N/A

  6. Say it in your own words

    What you like, or what you would change. A few words are plenty: "too long", or "I'd expect a price here".

    I'd make this headline shorter.

  7. Press "Save rating"

    It sits at the bottom of the panel, so scroll the panel down if you don't see it. Your pin stays on the page, and the top right corner reads "All feedback saved".

    Save rating

  8. Keep going

    Add as many pins as you like. "Page Notes", in the same panel, is for your reaction to the whole page. Changed your mind? Click a pin, edit it, and save again.

    123

Good to know

  • Use a computer. Echo is made for a computer screen. On a phone it says so, and lets you carry on if you like.
  • Name the page. Pins are filed under the home page. If your comment is about the About page, say so in the comment.
  • The live site is safe. Nothing you do in Echo changes meissner.services itself.
  • Stuck, or would rather just tell us? Reply to our email, or write to team@meissner.services.
