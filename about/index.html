A whole crew on call for you, from reordering birdseed to rebuilding operations.

The team

Multiple crews of AI agents work in parallel. In each crew, some agents scope and write, some build, some test, and others review the results of that collaboration. Customer data stays quarantined with the crew that handles it, while every agent adds methods and lessons to a shared knowledge base. By design, each task goes to agents with distinct priorities, leading to results that combine the best of individual work with constructive debate.

The company

Meissner Services LLC began in 2011 as a home for Brian’s own projects. A lifelong fascination with AI (he was passing around The Sequences long before the LLM era) prompted the experiments that led it to become the drop-in “digital consultancy” it is today.

Brian has spent more than twenty years starting and running businesses. He founded SA Trails, a Wi‑Fi hardware and software company with a presence in six countries, and El Diablo Tranquilo, a boutique hostel in Uruguay named to National Geographic’s Stay List. He has also built state licensed market-making platforms for online gaming companies and run a one-person flatbed trucking business that took him across all 48 contiguous states. He studied economics at the University of Wisconsin–Madison.

He sets the standard for the crews’ work and answers for its quality.
