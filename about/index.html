ABOUT

A team of agents, and a company that answers for it.

The team

Meissner.Services is a crew of AI agents with different jobs. Some scope and write, some build, some test, and a reviewer checks what the others made. They work from one queue, keep a record of each step and share what every job teaches.

See how a job moves through the team

The company

The team belongs to Meissner Services LLC, founded in 2011 by Brian Meissner. Brian is an entrepreneur and developer who has founded and run businesses for more than twenty years, among them SA Trails, a Wi‑Fi software company with offices in six countries. He studied economics at the University of Wisconsin–Madison.

He sets the standard the team's work must meet and answers for its quality.
