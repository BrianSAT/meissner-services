Setting up the new phone

A short video of the seven steps. Tap the arrow to play it. Pause it whenever you need to.

The same steps in writing

You need: the new phone, its charger, your mom's Wi-Fi name and password, and your own phone, for the step at the end.

  1. Plug it in and turn it on

    Plug the phone into its charger and hold the side button until the screen lights up.

  2. Go through the welcome screens
    • Pick English, then tap Start or Next.
    • When it asks for Wi-Fi, pick your mom's network and type the password.
    • If it offers to copy apps or data from another phone, tap "Don't copy" or Skip.
    • If it asks for a Google account, tap Skip. If it won't let you skip, stop and text Brian.
    • When it asks you to set a screen lock (PIN, pattern or fingerprint), choose Skip or None. Nobody will be holding this phone, so it must never ask for a code.
    • Say No or Skip to anything else that's optional, such as backups, extra apps or location.
  3. Help us find the phone's number

    From the new phone, text Brian, and text Brian's bots at (262) 315-2739, and say it's you. To send a text, open Messages (the blue speech bubble).

  4. Turn on Wi-Fi Calling

    In Settings, tap "Network & internet" (on some phones it says "Connections"), then "Calls & SMS" or "SIMs", and switch "Wi-Fi calling" on. If you can't find it, skip this step.

  5. Keep Messages running
    • Find the Messages app (a blue speech bubble). Open it once, and if it asks to be the default texting app, tap Yes.
    • Back in Settings, tap "Apps", then "Messages", then "Battery", and choose "Unrestricted". If you can't find it, skip this step.
  6. Connect the phone to us (the only step that's done together)
    • From your own phone, text Brian's bots at (262) 315-2739: "Ready to pair."
    • We'll text you back and walk you through it, one short step at a time. This part is new: there is no code to scan.
    • Follow each text on the new phone. The last one asks you to tap a small picture (an emoji) that we name.
    • We'll tell you when it's connected. Then you're done.
  7. Leave it alone

    Put the phone somewhere on a table, plugged into its charger and in reach of the Wi-Fi. Leave it on: no need to answer it, open it or turn it off. If it ever turns off, plug it in, turn it back on, and text Brian.

The voice in the video is computer-made. Stuck on any step? Text Brian.
