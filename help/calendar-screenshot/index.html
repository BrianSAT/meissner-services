Sending us a picture of your calendar

A short video. Tap the arrow to play it.

The same steps in writing

  1. Open your Calendar. Find an appointment that is showing up a day early, and leave it on the screen.
  2. Press two buttons at the same time and let go right away: the long button on the right side, and the button on the left side that turns the sound up. Don't hold them down.
  3. Your screen shows the picture. Tap the check mark at the top right, then tap Save to Photos.
  4. Go back to our text messages. Tap the plus sign at the bottom left, then tap Photos.
  5. Tap the picture of your calendar, then tap the green arrow to send it.

If something looks different

  • A screen with sliders appears: you held the buttons too long. Let go right away and tap Cancel. Then try again with a quicker press.
  • A small picture appears in the corner instead of filling the screen: you don't have to do anything. It saves by itself.
  • You see the word Done after you tap the picture: tap Done, then the green arrow.

The voice in the video is computer-made. Questions? Just text us back.
